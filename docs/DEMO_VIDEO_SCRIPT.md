# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

The gameplay footage is `docs/demo/demo-capture.mp4`: one continuous take in the
IWER XR simulator with emulated hand tracking. Times below are for that take;
the voiceover runs over it, with a short title before and an outro after.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Title card: *Bargain Bazaar. Haggle with your hands. Your style becomes an agent.* | "This is Bargain Bazaar, a night market you haggle in with nothing but your hands." |
| 0:05 | The camera settles at Mei Lantern's stall. | "Mei Lantern is a stock-pressure merchant. Her floor drops the longer a piece sits. The panel above her shows the asking price and the referee's veto line." |
| 0:10 | A pinch on the brass bead drags the price down to 56 coins. | "Pinch the bead and slide it to set a price." |
| 0:14 | A pinch in the air offers it. Lightning strikes from the referee, and the panel shows *VETO 0.65*. | "Offer a lowball and the fairness referee strikes it down. It scores every deal with Jain's fairness index, and the seller would have kept nothing." |
| 0:18 | An open palm faces Mei, and her ask drops from 150 to 127. | "An open palm asks her to move. She does." |
| 0:23 | The bead slides up to 126 and is offered. Mei accepts. | "A fair offer, and she accepts." |
| 0:28 | Two fists clasp above the table. The panel shows *Jain 1.00. A balanced split.* | "Clasp to seal. The referee scores it: balanced." |
| 0:33 | The camera turns to the Merchant Spirit panel. Its bars have moved. | "Every deal trains your Merchant Spirit. It copies your opening, your concessions, your patience, and your fairness target. After a lowball and a fair close, it has learned from both." |
| 0:45 | Calder Voss arrives. A palm turned back storms off. | "Calder is a floor hawk. Turn your palm back and walk away." |
| 0:52 | A finger presses *Step Away*. The camera turns to *While You Were Away*. | "Step away, or just take the headset off. Your Spirit keeps trading against the other merchants overnight, and every one of its deals faces the same referee." |
| 1:05 | The night report: sealed, vetoed, walked, profit, mean Jain, and rumors. | "When you come back, the night report is waiting: profits, losses, vetoes, and gossip from the row." |
| 1:20 | Outro card: live URL, *Meta VR Start 2026*, *MIT*. | "Bargain Bazaar runs in the browser on Quest, or in the XR simulator with no headset at all." |

The take is about 80 seconds. With the title and outro cards, the edit lands
near two minutes.
